## Interview positioning — 30-second answer

> I wanted to go beyond a DCF and learn how a financial sponsor actually underwrites a public-to-private deal. I selected the UK-listed Domino’s master franchisee and built a five-year LBO with an operating forecast, debt financing, cash sweep and sensitivity analysis. My initial scenario, with a 30% takeover premium and three-times EBITDA opening leverage, generated about 7.5% gross IRR, well below the 20% target. The lesson was that a stable cash-generative business is not automatically an attractive buyout at the prevailing public valuation. The hardest parts were the transaction bridge, lease accounting and debt roll-forward.
